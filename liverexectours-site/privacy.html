Privacy notice

What we collect when you book with us, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Liver Exec Tours is a chauffeur business based in Liverpool and is responsible for (the “data controller” of) the information described here. Contact: info@liverexectours.com or 07808 299060.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and (if you email us) email address.
  • Journey details: pick-up and drop-off addresses, dates, times, flight numbers, number of passengers and any notes you add.
  • For corporate accounts: company name, billing contact and the names of people who travel.
  • Payment records (we don't see or store full card numbers).

How we get it

You give it to us by phone, WhatsApp or email. The forms on this website don't store or send anything themselves — they put your details into a message that opens in your own WhatsApp or email app, and you choose whether to send it.

Why we use it

  • To quote for, arrange and carry out your journey, and to contact you about it (to perform our contract with you).
  • To invoice and keep accounts (legal obligation).
  • To ask for a Google review after your trip, and to let regular shuttle passengers know about new runs (legitimate interests — you can tell us to stop at any time).

Who we share it with

Only with the driver doing your journey, and with services we use to run the business: WhatsApp (Meta), our email provider, and our accountant. We never sell your information.

This website

  • Address search and route preview use Google Maps. What you type in an address box is sent to Google to suggest addresses and draw the route. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

Booking details for as long as we need them to look after your booking and any follow-up, and invoices for six years for tax purposes. Then we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to us using it. Contact us using the details above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

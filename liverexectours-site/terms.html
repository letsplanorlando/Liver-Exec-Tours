The small print

Terms of booking

Plain-English terms for private journeys, shuttle seats and corporate accounts.

Who we are

Liver Exec Tours is a family-run chauffeur business based in Liverpool, fully licensed and insured. Licence details are available on request. Contact us on 07808 299060 or info@liverexectours.com.

Quotes and bookings

  • Enquiries sent through this website, WhatsApp or email are requests, not bookings. A booking is confirmed only when we confirm it to you in writing (WhatsApp, text or email).
  • Quotes are based on the journey details you give us. If the journey changes (extra stops, longer waiting, a different vehicle), the price may change and we'll tell you before it does.
  • Please tell us the correct number of passengers and luggage. We can refuse to carry more passengers than the vehicle is licensed for.

Prices and payment

  • Your quote sets out what’s included, including VAT where it applies and any airport parking or drop-off charges.
  • For private bookings, we’ll tell you how and when to pay when we confirm your booking. Corporate accounts are invoiced monthly on terms agreed with you.

Changes and cancellations (private journeys)

  • Need to change or cancel? Tell us as early as you can. Any cancellation charge will be made clear when we confirm your booking.
  • If we have to cancel, you get a full refund of anything you've paid, and we'll try to arrange an alternative.

Waiting time and flight delays

For airport pick-ups we ask for your flight number when you book and track your flight. With a flight number we wait until you land, at no extra charge. For other pick-ups, please be ready at the agreed time; if extra waiting is needed, we’ll tell you about any charge before it applies.

Shuttle and event seats

  • Shuttle and event seats are sold per person. Each run has a minimum number of seats (13 of 16 on the Sprinter, 5 of 7 on the Vito), shown on each event page. The run is confirmed once that minimum is reached.
  • If the minimum isn't reached by the cut-off shown for that event, the run is cancelled and nothing is charged.
  • Reserving a seat is free. Once a run is confirmed we send a payment link; seats not paid for by the deadline on the link may be released. If a run is cancelled, nothing is charged.
  • If you need to cancel a confirmed seat, tell us as soon as you can and we’ll talk you through your options.
  • If an event is postponed, your seat moves to the new date or you can have a refund. If it's cancelled, you get a full refund.
  • The shuttle leaves at the advertised time. We can't wait for late passengers on a shared run.
  • Luggage: one carry-on bag per seat. There's no room for large cases on a shared run.
  • Private bookings of a whole shuttle: all seats are sold to one group, the run is guaranteed, and the group chooses one pick-up and drop-off point in Liverpool.

On board

  • Seat belts must be worn. No smoking or vaping.
  • We may refuse to carry anyone who is abusive or a danger to others. A cleaning charge may apply if the vehicle is soiled.
  • Child seats are available on request — please ask when booking.

Lost property

We’ll keep anything left in a vehicle safe. Contact us to arrange collection.

Our responsibility

We'll get you there with reasonable care and skill. We're not responsible for delays caused by things outside our control, such as traffic, road closures, weather or breakdowns, but we'll do everything we can to keep you moving. Nothing in these terms limits liability that can't legally be limited.

Complaints

If something isn't right, tell us straight away on 07808 299060 or info@liverexectours.com and we'll put it right where we can.
